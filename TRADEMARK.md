  close. Call it something else and note "based on Lumen" — that is accurate
  and welcome.
- Use "Lumen" or "Syne" in your company name, product name, domain name, or
  app store listing in a way that implies we built or endorse it
- Use our logos in a modified form, or in a way that suggests affiliation
- Register "Lumen" or a confusingly similar mark for analytics software
- Imply that a hosted service you operate is official, certified, or
  supported by SyneHQ

## Naming context

"Lumen" is a common English word and an SI unit. Other unrelated projects and
companies use it, including a PHP micro-framework and a US telecom. Our claim
is limited to product analytics and event ingestion software, and we make no
claim on the word generally.

If you are building something adjacent and unsure whether your name collides,
just ask — we would rather answer an email than send a lawyer's letter.
